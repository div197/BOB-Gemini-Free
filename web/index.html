        🧠 How LLM Tokenization Works: Language models do not read raw letters or words — they process tokens (frequently occurring character chunks). In English, 1 token ≈ 4 chars (~0.75 words). In Devanagari & Indic scripts, complex conjuncts (अक्षर) are grouped into subwords. This is why character-level puzzles like "strawberry" require step-by-step reasoning!
